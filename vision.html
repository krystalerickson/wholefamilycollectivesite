Vision, Mission & Values

Our Vision

A world where every family navigating developmental delays or disabilities has the knowledge, confidence, relationships, and resources to flourish through every stage of life.

Our Mission

We empower caregivers of children with developmental delays and disabilities in Kenya through evidence-based interventions, community partnerships, and sustainable support systems that help families thrive.

Our Values

Integrity

We're radically honest, honor our commitments, and admit mistakes quickly.

Dignity & Agency

We design alongside caregivers and communities, share ownership, and respect local expertise.

Rigor

We measure what matters, test assumptions, and change course when the evidence changes.

Courage

We choose the harder right over the easier wrong, and don't shy away from difficult problems, decisions, or conversations.

Humility

We stay curious, seek feedback early, and recognize there's something to learn from everyone.

Collaboration

We share knowledge generously, partner openly, and leave every system stronger than we found it.

Families can't flourish alone. Neither can we.

If you share our conviction that families deserve better, let’s build together.
