Our Team

The people building Whole Family Collective from the ground up.

LN

Linda Ndungu

Co-Founder & Co-Executive Director

Linda is a Kenya-based operator and strategist with experience building and scaling businesses and programs across East Africa. She most recently served as Country Manager for Bolt's ride-hailing business in Kenya, leading operations across more than ten cities.

Previously, she spent nine years at Deloitte and Instiglio working across strategy, operations, and program implementation, with earlier experience at Coca-Cola. She holds a B.Com in Marketing from the University of Nairobi and is a Certified Public Accountant (CPA-K).

KE

Krystal Erickson

Co-Founder & Co-Executive Director

Krystal is a US-based founder, operator, and strategist, based in Washington, DC, with a background spanning healthcare and technology at Samsung, Oracle, two early-stage startups, and a boutique professional services firm. Before pivoting her career into global health, she co-founded an AI healthcare fraud detection startup through Techstars AI Health. Her education includes an MBA from MIT Sloan School of Business, a B.S. in Engineering: Product Design from Stanford University, and currently, a Master of Public Health at Imperial College London.

Her connection to this work is personal. Her younger brother, Calvin, has profound non-verbal autism. Growing up alongside disability shaped her conviction that families need support alongside their children. Through years of volunteering, she's come to recognize that while environments and circumstances change, the core needs of caregivers are similar worldwide: to be seen, heard, and supported through all the blessings and challenges that raising a child brings. She views this work not just as a career, but as a long-term calling.

Families can't flourish alone. Neither can we.

If you have lived experience, local knowledge, implementation expertise, research, relationships, funding, or simply a conviction that families deserve better, we'd love to hear from you.
